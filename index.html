About

Profile

I am a Tenured Associate Professor in the School of Physics Science and Engineering at Tongji University. My research bridges wave physics, topology and artificial intelligence, with a focus on spin angular momentum of classical waves, topological physics, phononic circuit chips and AI for science.

Previously, I was an Eric and Wendy Schmidt AI in Science Postdoctoral Fellow and Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. Beyond physics, I am a fingerstyle guitar player and an open-source enthusiast.

E-mail: longyangphysics@tongji.edu.cn

Research Interests

Directions

AI for Science

Unsupervised learning of physical phases, inverse design, and machine-learning methods for wave and topological systems.

Spin Angular Momentum of Classical Waves

Theory and applications of acoustic, elastic and photonic spin in classical wave systems.

Phononic Circuit Chips

Chip-scale phononic circuits for wave manipulation and information processing.

Topological Physics

Theory and applications of topological phases, including non-Hermitian and time-crystalline systems.

Photonic / Phononic Crystals

Metamaterials, bound states in the continuum and near-field wave devices.
